Privacy

Send-Date Ledger is designed so your invoice chronology stays on your device.

What stays local

Invoice references, client names, amounts, dates, notes, export snapshots, license tokens, and attached PDFs are stored in your browser using IndexedDB or local storage. We do not receive or synchronize this data. Backups are created on your device and downloaded only when you ask.

Encrypted backups

Encrypted backups use AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase. Encryption happens in your browser. We do not know or recover the passphrase.

Purchases and license checks

If you buy Studio, checkout is hosted by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record. Their systems process payment and contact details under their own policies. This app sends your license token to Sociobot at most once per day to verify whether it remains active. It does not send ledger contents.

Analytics and network use

The app contains no advertising, behavioral analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or runtime scripts. Apart from a license verification you initiate or have activated, the ledger needs no server connection. Your hosting provider may retain standard security logs such as IP address and request time.

Your control

Export a plain or encrypted backup whenever you like. To erase local data, clear this site's storage in your browser. Uninstalling the PWA alone may not clear browser data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
