Privacy

This policy explains what the app stores and when it uses the network.

Invoice records stay in your browser

Invoice references, client names, amounts, dates, notes, monthly CSV exports, and attached PDFs use browser storage. The app does not synchronize them.

Demo records are separate

The demo uses its own browser database and license keys. Starting for real clears demo changes and opens the separate real database.

Backups

Plain and encrypted backups are created in your browser. Encrypted backups use AES-256-GCM and a key derived from your passphrase. The app does not store that passphrase.

License checks

If you restore a PDF storage license, the app sends only that token to Sociobot for verification. A successful result is reused for up to one day.

Analytics and hosting logs

The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts, or third-party scripts. The hosting provider may retain security logs such as an IP address and request time.

Remove your data

Download a backup first if needed. Then clear this site’s storage in your browser. Uninstalling the app alone may not clear browser data.

Contact

Email privacy@sociobot.in with privacy questions.
